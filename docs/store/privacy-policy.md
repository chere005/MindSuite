This policy covers the CalMind and MealMind apps (iOS, Android, web, desktop) and the sync service they use.

## Using the apps without an account

You can use both apps without creating an account. Your data (events, notes, habits, recipes, shopping and pantry lists) stays on your device. We receive nothing from the app in this mode.

Photos: if you choose a recipe-card photo, the app reads its text on your device. The photo is not uploaded. The app asks only for the photos you pick; it does not use the camera or microphone.

## Using an account (optional)

An account lets you sync between devices and share with other people through our server. With an account we collect and store:

- **Username** and **email address** (the email is used only for password reset and account notices).
- **Password**, stored only as a salted hash.
- **Your records** (the content you create). They are encrypted at rest on the server.
- **Sign-in sessions** (so you stay signed in until you sign out).
- **IP addresses**, used only for rate limiting and abuse prevention, and kept for a short time (`<retention period>`).
- **A usage log of action names** (for example "sign in", "sync"), without record content, used to run and debug the service.

## What we do not do

No tracking, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no sale or sharing of your data with third parties. The server is run by the developer; `<hosting provider>` hosts it as a processor and does not use the data for its own purposes.

## Retention and deletion

Your records and account details are kept while the account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings, Account) and on the web account page `<account page URL>`. Deletion removes your username, email, records and sessions from the server. Server backups containing them expire within `<backup retention>`. Log entries (action names, IP addresses) are removed on the retention schedule above.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

## Your choices and rights

You can export your data from the app, correct it in the app, or ask us to delete it. Contact `<support contact>` for any request, including access requests under applicable law (for example GDPR or CCPA).

## Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
