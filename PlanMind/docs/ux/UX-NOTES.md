# PlanMind UX notes: Day, Week, Month

From the 2026-10-30 wireframe sessions with the owner. Wireframes are static HTML previews in this folder (they load Tabler icons from a CDN and use stand-in theme variables, so open them in a browser).

Source: three photos of the owner's paper planner. Day = Monday schedule 7A–8P, Priorities 1–5, To-do with circles, Notes. Week = Weekly plan with Priorities lines and To-do lines. Month = Mon–Sun grid, Priorities and To-do in a left column, Notes in the leftover cells of the last row.

## Approved decisions

- **Desktop:** sidebar (Month/Week/Day toggle, Today button, today ±7 list) beside the panel. Day = Schedule 7a–8p (the AM page, below), Notes, Priorities 1–5 (typed lines), To-do with circles. Week = Priorities (text) and To-do (ruled lines like the Day's since 2026-10-31, below; the wireframe draws circle rows). Month = 6-row grid of the month's chores (chips in the days, 0.15.0, below), Priorities and To-do in a left column, Notes in the leftover cells.
- **Phone top bar:** hamburger, then ‹ date ›, then a round list-picker button. On a Day opened from the Month, Back to month takes the hamburger's place (2026-10-31, below). Tapping the date opens a selector sheet: calendar on Day, Monday–Sunday week list on Week, month grid on Month.
- **Phone bottom tab bar:** Day = Schedule (default) | To-do | Notes. Week = Priorities | To-do. Month = Calendar | Priorities | To-do | Notes | Chores (5-tab bar shows only the active tab's label; the Calendar tab is the grid with a bar for each chore of a day and the month's chores listed under it, 0.15.0).
- **Day phone tabs fit one screen, no scrolling:** cap characters per priority, number and length of to-dos, and notes length, with a live counter (examples in the wireframe: 6 to-dos, 400 notes characters, 18 characters per priority; the owner set 13 to-dos on 2026-10-31; a schedule entry has no row-sized cap since 2026-11-01: its block shows as many lines as fit, then an ellipsis). Week and Month phone tabs have no caps and scroll.
- **Day priorities** are five typed lines on the To-do tab (confirmed).
- **Both view uses lanes.** Priorities and Schedule entries match by line (priority number or time). The signed-in person is the left lane, the other person the right; an entry added as Both is one purple entry spanning both lanes. To-do has no line to match, so it stays one list with a colored circle per owner.
- **Row growth:** desktop, Week and Month rows grow to the taller lane. Day phone rows never grow, hence the caps.
- **Add as Both** writes one linked entry to both lists. It shows in the owner's, the partner's and the Both view. Editing or deleting it changes both. Checking off a Both to-do completes it for both (confirmed).
- **Colors:** defaults: partner yellow, owner green, Both purple, using readable tints per theme. In the wireframes these are the host tokens `text/bg/border-warning`, `-success` and `-pro`. Each person's list color is a household setting; **the Both color is each person's own (the owner, 2026-11-09, #138)**: the owner and the partner each pick it under Manage lists, see their own pick everywhere Both is colored (items, the picker button, the Day's CalMind cards), and the other person's views do not move. Until someone picks, the household default (purple) applies. The linked-priority text cap stays **18** characters (decided 2026-11-09, #138). Names always appear in lane headers, so color is never the only cue.

## Decisions of 2026-10-31 (the owner)

- **One user selector at the top, no For control:** the round list picker in the top bar is the only place a person says who new items are for (the base list, or both of us under Both; overlays never decide; a tapped priority cell uses its own lane, and so does a schedule slot, except the middle third of the Both view's schedule, which is the linked entry). Editing an item never changes its owners; a schedule entry changes them only by dragging it (or Alt+Left and Alt+Right) across the thirds of the Both view; any other item is re-added under the other selection. The picker menu also holds, below the lists, a Chores switch on a desktop's Day only and "The rhythm…" on the Day and the Month (not the Week); a phone's menu has no Chores row (0.15.0, below).
- **Day to-dos: 13 open per list** (was 6, an example to tune in the wireframe). Week and Month caps are unchanged.
- **To-do is 13 ruled slots (the owner: "make all the empty slots for the todos visible so it looks more like the original page"):** open to-dos fill them top-down, the rest are blank lines with a faint empty circle, the first blank line is the add field (no separate add row; a tap on any blank line goes to it), the "list is full" message shows when 13 are open. Done to-dos stay in the same list, crossed out, stacked at the bottom of it, under the blank lines (2026-11-01 and the same day's correction, below).
- **Unchecked day to-dos carry over to today (the owner: "unchecked todos from previous days carry over to today, don't show on future days"):** display only. Today's Day view lists the open day to-dos dated before today, oldest first, then today's own, each with a muted "from Mon Oct 26" chip; they stay on their own day; no later day and no other past day shows them. Done, week and month to-dos never carry. Complete, edit (the day field is the one way to move it), reopen and delete act on the real record. On today the cap of 13 counts them (server and page); a day with more than 13 open shows 13 rows and "+N more". A to-do ticked from today's list is done on today (`doneOn`, the owner, 2026-11-02: it "only shows checked for the day it was accomplished, previous days show it unchanged/unchecked"): checked in today's done pile, still with its chip; its own day shows it unchecked with a small "→ Tue 3" chip pointing at today and a circle that does nothing; it no longer carries, and unchecking brings it back. A to-do ticked on its own day is done there. Spec 9.2.
- **New schedule entries start on the quarter hour** the tap is in, not the hour or half hour.
- **Desktop Day fits the window:** a 56px top bar, a one-row page header, hour rows that flex to the window (22px at least); a scroll bar only when the window is shorter than the content. Notes stays under the schedule, as the approved wireframe draws it.
- **The account is one menu:** dot, name, chevron, with Settings (the suite's four themes, saved per account) and Sign out, on the desktop top bar and in the phone drawer. No page footer text; the sidebar footer keeps "Alex & Sam".
- **A sidebar button** (left end of the desktop top bar) hides and shows the sidebar; the choice is saved per person with the picker selection. The view switch and the date list go with the sidebar (the page header's arrows and date button stay).
- **Both priorities by pointer (settled 2026-11-01):** the thirds below answered it. In the Both view a blank priority line (and a blank to-do line, and a schedule slot) is cut in thirds, and the middle third makes the one linked item for both of us.
- **CalMind all-day events (the owner, 2026-11-09, #171):** a compact read-only strip above the schedule, below the lane names, in both AM and PM pages and on a desktop and a phone. Planner day D is D 7:00 to D+1 7:00, so an all-day date D belongs to planner day D, and a multi-day all-day event (CalMind's `endDate`, inclusive, at most 60 days) is on each day from its date through its end date. It takes two 22px rows at most and no height at all on a day with none; a chip sits in its owner's lane or, for an event on both people's calendars (same words) or the designated Both calendar, across both lanes in this person's Both color; a lane with more than fits shows one fewer chip and `+N` (tooltip and label list what it hides). It is opaque and tinted like the timed CalMind cards, never a control, and the CalMind button hides it with them.
- **Edit in place, no content windows (the owner: "dont bring up a window for editing events or reminders or anything, always edit in place"):** a to-do, priority, entry or list row becomes a field where it stands (Enter or the focus leaving saves, Escape cancels, Delete is two presses in the row; an entry is the exception: it has no Delete in its editor, it is deleted by a swipe and a tap, below); the entry keeps its handles and nothing else hangs over the schedule (the owner, 2026-11-01: "Get rid of the hovering stuff completely .. delete with a swipe and tap delete": the strip of span, counter, Delete and notes that hung beside the handles is gone); editing an entry is only its text, and its time and length are set by dragging the block or a handle (the owner, 2026-10-31: "the only way to set time and duration is by dragging the box, editing should only be the text itself"), with Alt+Arrow and Alt+Shift+Arrow as the keyboard path. **Confirming a delete (the owner, 2026-10-31):** the trash icon just turns red on the first press, like CalMind's; no "Confirm delete" text and no Cancel; it forgets itself after 2.5 seconds. **Adding as Both on the schedule (the owner, 2026-10-31):** with the Both view the schedule is cut in thirds; a tap or click in the middle third makes the linked entry for both, the left third is the left person's lane and the right third the right person's. Settings, the date sheets, the picker and the account menu stay popups. Spec 9.1.
- **Lists and the To-do scroll (2026-10-31, after review):** "Manage lists" was a window around the list rows (edited in place); since 2026-11-09 (#139) it is an in-page panel over the page under the top bar (no dialog layer: not modal, Escape or Close gives the page back, focus on its heading, the page behind inert); a desktop's To-do list (open rows, the add line, then the done ones) scrolls inside itself when it has more rows than the window leaves lines. (An entry has no counter or Delete in its editor since 2026-11-01: see Edit in place.)
- **Week and Month to-do are ruled lines like the Day's (2026-10-31):** at least 13 lines on a Week and 8 on a Month (more when more are open), a colored circle per open row and a faint one on each blank line, the add field on the first blank line (a tap on a blank line or on its circle goes to it), the Add button only once there is text, no "Nothing here yet", the owner's name for screen readers only. Done rows are the last lines of the same list, crossed out (under the blank lines, see the correction below). Spec 9.5.
- **Done to-dos are never hidden (the owner, 2026-11-01: "don't hide done todos, put crossed out items at the bottom"):** a done to-do stays in the same list as the open ones, crossed out and dim, on the Day (desktop and phone), the Week and the Month: no "Done (n)" button, heading or block, no separate list. Checking a row sends it down, unchecking brings it back; done rows use up blank lines but never count against the 13 open ones. The Day's list scrolls inside itself when open and done rows are more than the 13 lines (the page and, on a phone, the tab never scroll); the Week's and Month's list simply grows. Judged on screen: the checked circles keep their owner color and the crossed-out titles are dim, so a run of finished rows reads as quietly done and not as clutter. Spec 9.8. *Where the done rows sit was corrected the same day (0.10.0, see the next entry): the first build put them right after the open rows, which is not what "at the bottom" meant.*
- **Done to-dos stack at the bottom of the page (the owner, 2026-11-01, about 0.9.0: "checked todos stack at the bottom of the list rather than the top of the list"):** the ruled page is the open rows from the top, then the blank ruled lines (the add field the first of them), then the done rows crossed out as its LAST lines, on the Day (13 lines), the Week (13) and the Month (8). The page keeps its number of lines: the blank lines are what the open and done rows leave (`max(0, lines - open - done)`, at least the add line on the Week and Month), so a finished row takes its place in the pile at the bottom and frees no line, and a new open row takes the first blank line. With more rows than lines nothing is hidden: the one add line sits between the open rows and the pile and the list grows (the Week and Month) or scrolls inside itself (the Day). Everything else is as before: crossed out and dim, the circle reopens it, the 13 limit counts open rows only, "+N more" is open overflow only, the thirds work on the blank lines that remain, a row is edited and deleted where it stands. Judged on screen at 1360x900, 375x667 and 320x568: the pile at the bottom under faint-circled blank ruled lines (the first showing "Add something…") reads as a finished stack, not as missing rows; on a phone shorter than about 700px the 13 lines do not fit the tab, so the pile is under the fold of the list and one scroll away (the list scrolls inside the tab, the tab never does). Spec 9.8.
- **A list that scrolls stays where it is (2026-11-01, after review):** with open and done rows beyond the lines the Day's To-do list scrolls inside itself, and checking or reopening a row, opening, cancelling or saving an edit, or the other person's change arriving never throws it back to the top; the row you touched stays under your finger and the keyboard's place (the edit field, the add field, the row's button) is brought into view. The keyboard's focus ring of every control in the list is whole, also on the first and the last row.
- **Month side column, Priorities up to two thirds (the owner, 2026-11-02: "give 2/3 of the vertical space to priorities, and if there's not 8 lines, make the card smaller.. show at least 8 lines"):** on a desktop the Priorities section is as tall as its cards need, at most two thirds of the column; To-do has the rest and its list scrolls inside itself when that is shorter than its eight lines (the column scrolls only when both are at their floors). A card's box grows line by line with its words (two lines when empty, no resize grip), a short card leaves its room to the other, two long ones share evenly, a box past its share scrolls inside itself and shows a shadow along its bottom edge until it is scrolled to the end. The card's chrome is slim (name and Reload saved on one row, count, state and a 28px Save on the other; with a newer version saved elsewhere, Reload saved and Keep mine take a row of their own under the sentence), so a full card shows 8 or more lines at 1360x900 with both full and at 1280x800 with the other short. Week, the Month's Notes and the phone are unchanged.
- **Tapping a Month day opens the Day with Back to month (2026-10-31):** a Back button in the top bar returns to the Month of the day the Day is on by then; choosing a view by hand forgets it. On a phone it takes the hamburger's place, so the drawer (view switch, dates, Today, account menu) is reachable only after Back: accepted. After Back the keyboard goes to the date (phone) or the sidebar button (desktop). Spec 9.4.
- **The schedule has two pages, AM and PM (the owner, 2026-10-31: "AM should be exactly as it was before, PM should start at the hours that AM ended at", then "PM should be 9pm-7am"):** on a desktop and a phone, behind an AM/PM switch. AM is the old day, 7a to 9p, 14 rows (on a phone each is about 4px shorter than before, because the 44px switch row sits above the grid). PM is the night, 9p to 7a, ten rows at that same height (the box keeps AM's size and PM leaves its last four rows empty). A planner day now runs 7:00 to 7:00 the next morning, so an entry at 2a belongs to the evening's date and is on that date's PM page. The page always opens on AM and the choice is kept while the page is open. The switch is compact at the right end of the Schedule heading on a desktop and a row of its own on a phone. An entry across 9p is cut flat at each page edge with no handle there; new entries, drags, handles and the keyboard stay on, or partly on, the page shown, and the page never leaves the entry being edited. Not built: pulling an entry across 9p by a handle (only the keyboard resize reaches past it). Spec 9.3. This replaced the phone-only split at noon of 0.5.0.
- **44px exceptions (2026-10-31):** the phone's AM/PM buttons are 44px (a compact switch on a desktop). The Week and Month Add button is 28px on a phone, a small inline button like the Day's Add (26px); Enter adds too.
- **Desktop Month chip targets under 24px (0.15.0, deliberate):** a chip of the desktop calendar is 18px high with a 14px circle, below the 24px minimum (WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8) and at a 19px pitch, so that a number of 24px and three chips fit an 88px row at 1280x800 with the sidebar shown. It is a mouse-first, dense view: the same check and owner cycle are in the Day's Chores list (rows about 32px) and, for a finger, in the phone's list (46px rows). The date number is 24px, and the empty part of the box opens the day as well.
- **Dragging works on the entry itself** (the interaction draft below, for schedule entries): press and move on a desktop, press and hold 0.4 s on a touch screen, 15-minute steps, Escape cancels, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move 15 minutes. Priorities and to-dos are not draggable yet.

## Chores (the owner, 2026-11-02; first built inside the planner, rejected, rebuilt as a view of its own, then folded into the Day and the Month at 0.15.0)

- **Always shared:** chores ignore the picker (owner, partner, Both, a custom list: every chore shows to both accounts). They are rules with per-date marks, not to-dos: they never take to-do lines or carry over.
- **0.15.0, desktop (the owner, 2026-11-02 late: "just put the chores in that form on the existing calendar and remove the dots for things that are scheduled.. the calendar will just be used for chores in month view", then "on desktop, when i select chores, just replace the daily todo list with the chores todo list"):** there is no page of its own on a desktop. The **Month** calendar always shows the month's chores (below). The **Day** has a "Chores" switch in the picker menu (a house and a toggle, after the list rows, Day only): on, the Day's To-do section (the right column under Priorities) is titled "Chores" and lists the day's chores in the same box and height, scrolling inside itself when long; off, the to-dos are back. Schedule, Priorities and Notes stay as they are and keep following the list selection; the switch never touches the selection or the stored data (it is app state for the session, and survives a release reload in the URL hash). The **Week** has no chores on a desktop. The picker button stays the lists' pie. "The rhythm…" is in the menu on the Day and the Month, not the Week (desktop and phone).
- **Phone:** Chores is the last tab of each bar (Day 4 with all labels, Week 3 with all labels, Month 5 with the chosen label only). It takes the page over (the Chores view): the same bar stays at the bottom with Chores chosen, another tab brings the planner back on that tab, and the round picker is a grey, locked house named "Chores" (no menu, and the menu never has a Chores row). The arrows, the date sheet and the drawer's Day / Week / Month change the period and stay on Chores. The flag is one: a window widened past 760px while it is on lands on the planner, whose Day then has the switch on. In the other direction the Day's switch is the Day's alone: a window narrowed to a phone with the switch on shows the Chores view on the Day, but the Week and the Month stay on their planner tabs (the phone's own Chores tab is what makes them Chores pages; `state.choresTab`).
- **Day:** open turns first, then "Missed" (this chore week's missed turns, each with "Fri" and "Let go"), or on a Friday "Catch-up" (Sunday to Thursday, each with a "from Tue" chip and "Let go"), then rows sent to Friday or let go by hand (dim, a chip that takes it back), then the done rows crossed out. A weekly, Week A / Week B or monthly row wears a quiet badge ("Weekly", "Week A", "Week B", "Monthly": dim text, thin border, no owner color) at its right end, before its "from Tue" chip and its buttons; a daily row has none, and the Week and the Month keep their own chips. Saturday is "Day off". Phone: rows 34px shrinking to 30px, past that the list scrolls inside itself. Desktop: the same rows in the To-do box when the Day's Chores switch is on (rows about 32px, the box scrolls).
- **Owner:** the circle is in the owner's color and checks the turn; the title is a button that cycles that one turn partner, owner, Both. In The rhythm the circle cycles the rule's default. A turn whose owner equals the rule's has no mark.
- **Week:** "Week A" or "Week B" (a phone's head line; a desktop's header), a grid of the daily chores (a tap on today's or an earlier day's cell toggles done, today's column marked), then each day's own chores; Friday is "Catch-up", Saturday "Day off". (Phone only since 0.15.0; a desktop has no Week chores.)
- **Month (0.15.0, replaces the 2026-11-02 pass):** the planner's Month calendar shows the month's chores (monthly and Week A / Week B turns, the same set the phone's Chores Month list has), and the dots and counts for what is scheduled are gone. The grid is uniform: seven equal columns (Saturday as wide as Friday, a quiet diagonal hatch in or out of the month and no "Day off" text), six equal rows (below), Week A rows lightly highlighted on the days of the month, Week B rows plain, days outside the month transparent, no A / B column, no letters, no legend; today's number is a filled accent circle and its cell keeps its highlight. A day is a box, never one big button: its number is the button that opens that Day (with "Back to month" as before), and the empty part of the box opens it too. Desktop: each day lists its turns as owner-tinted chips (a circle that checks, the title cycling that turn's owner, done struck and faded, let go dimmed, missed with the warning edge); a title that does not fit ends in an ellipsis on its one line (its whole text is the button's tooltip), a turn let go or sent to Friday keeps at least 4.5em of title and its undo chip ("Let go", "→ Fri") goes on a line of its own under it when the cell is too narrow for both, and a day with more chips than fit scrolls inside its box; the date number is 24px and sits 4px from the box's top and left so today's focus ring is whole inside it; the page still fits 1360x900 and 1280x800 with no scroll bar, and the side column is as in 0.14.0. Phone (the owner picked option A of the mockup): the Calendar tab is the same grid with a thin bar in the owner's color (the tint's text color, 3:1 against every cell in all four themes; done, sent away and let go ones faded) for each chore of a day, three at most and "+n" for the rest, and under the grid the month's chores as the Chores page lists them (Monthly, then each week with its A / B chores, date chips, circle, owner cycle), all in the tab's one scroll; a tap on a day opens it as before. Every-day and every-week chores are on the Day only.
- **Month, six equal rows, Notes compact (the owner, 2026-11-02: "make the size of the calendar uniform.. drop the a/b column and just indicate with highlights"):** all six week rows are one height in every month at every desktop window (88px at least, the page never scrolls for them); the Notes in the sixth row's leftover cells take no height from it: "Notes" at the left on the first card's name line, a card per lane in the Priorities cards' chrome (name and Reload saved on one line, count and a 24px Save on the other, the word Saved beside the name until there is a draft) around a box of about two lines (more when the row is taller, 13px/1.5 as ever) that scrolls inside itself with a shadow along its bottom edge; a card has three rows in every state and never a fourth (a notice takes the place of what it makes useless: over the limit a "Too long" cue beside the count and no Save, saved elsewhere a "Newer version" cue where the count was and Keep mine where Save was, Reload saved beside the name; the sentence stays as the status and a tooltip); a card keeps 156px, so with three or more lanes (or a window under about 1260px) the strip scrolls sideways inside the block, snaps, keeps its place across a redraw and shows a card whole when the keyboard reaches it.
- **The rhythm:** "The rhythm…" under "Manage lists…" in the picker menu opens a dialog of five groups (Daily, Weekly, Week A, Week B, Monthly), each rule edited in place (title; days; weeks or 1st to Last and months; two-press delete), an add line per group (a new rule is for both of us, whatever the picker shows), and a "This week is A | B" switch. Turns let go by time (a later turn of the same title, or past the catch-up Friday) are not listed anywhere.

## Interaction draft (needs the owner's approval)

- **Desktop:** drag a schedule block vertically; it snaps to 15 minutes, shows start–end while moving, auto-scrolls near the edge, and lands on the nearest free quarter-hour in its lane. Dragging changes the owner only across the thirds of the Both view (below). Drag priorities and to-dos to reorder (accent line shows the landing spot; numbers update on drop). Double-click edits in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels); single click selects.
- **Phone:** press and hold (about 0.4 s, haptic on lift and on each 15-minute step), then drag. Tap edits (who it is for is the picker at the top, not a control in the editor).
- **Schedule event in text-edit mode (the owner, 2026-10-30):** while an event's text is being edited, show drag icons (handles) at its start and end edges to change the start time or the end/duration. This replaces the draft's bottom-edge-only resize on desktop and gives the phone a way to resize, since press-and-hold only moves a block. Handles snap to 15 minutes, show start–end while dragging, and save with the text in the same write.
- **Time pill while editing (the owner, 2026-11-02: "show start and end time as a small hover when editing a schedule item"):** an entry being edited shows its span ("7:30p – 8:30p", 11px, in its owner's tone, AA in all four themes) in a small pill over the middle of its block, above the start grip, or below the end grip where the grid has no room above (an entry at 7a); it takes no room, cannot be pressed, is hidden from a screen reader (the block already says the time), follows the grips, Alt+Arrow and Alt+Shift+Arrow, and gives way to the drag's own bubble while a grip or the block is dragged. It is the one thing that hangs over an entry being edited (0.8.0's no-strip rule stands for everything else: no counter, no hints).
- **Drag across owner / Both / partner (the owner, 2026-11-02: "allow dragging from Alex to both to Sam"):** in the Both view with exactly the two people's lanes (the view the schedule is cut in thirds for new entries), a lifted drag changes whom the entry is for: the third its centre is in, carried by the pointer's sideways travel (so a press anywhere on a block and a drag straight up or down changes nothing) — left the signed-in person, middle both (one linked entry across both lanes), right the other. The block previews in its new lanes and the owner's color, the bubble adds "· Both" while that is a change, and it lands on the nearest free quarter hour for the new owners (a linked entry needs both lanes); where none is free the preview stays at the last good place. The drop is one `edit` with the new owners and start (an entry being edited changes its draft, written on Enter). A press that goes left before it lifts is still the swipe to Delete. Alt+Left and Alt+Right do it from the keyboard ("Dinner is for both of you, 6:45p – 7:30p."). In any other view (one list, or a list on top) a drag never changes owners.
- **Schedule model change:** entries carry a start time in 15-minute steps and a length. Length is adjustable via the edit-mode handles, and the default is half an hour (the owner, 2026-11-10; it was an hour from 2026-10-31 and 30 minutes before that; existing entries keep their lengths). The minimum was 30 minutes and is 15 since 2026-11-01 (the owner: "allow dragging down to 15 mins"); a new entry was still an hour, or half an hour when no hour was free, never a quarter, until 2026-11-02, when it began to fit where the pointer is (the owner, at a gap that held 30 minutes: "cursor in the red X should put a thing for 30 mins there or even 15 if that's all that's available"): if the pointer's quarter hour is free in every lane the entry is for, the entry is the default length (30 minutes since 2026-11-10; an hour before), in the free stretch around the pointer, starting at the pointer's quarter and earlier only as far as it takes to fit; only when that quarter is taken in one of those lanes (the middle third over a time when one person is busy) is it the nearest free start for that length. The same for one lane, a lane third and the middle third; the Add button (no pointer) still takes the nearest free start to 9a by the same order of lengths.
- **Priorities reorder:** dragging one person's priority reorders only that person's list. A Both entry spans the lanes only while it sits on the same line in both lists; otherwise it shows as two purple half-lane cells.
- **Saving:** a drop is one write with the record's revision; if stale, snap back and refresh. Keyboard: Alt+Up/Down moves 15 minutes (schedule) or one line (lists); Enter edits.

## Known issues in the draft

- Legend items 6 and 7 (to-do hover grip, to-do edit) have no matching badge on the frame.
- On the phone, 30-minute blocks are 12 px tall (a quarter hour 6 px, its words set at 9 px and allowed to touch the border); a block shows as many lines of its words as its height holds (`--lines`, set by `fitLines` from the block's height and kept by a ResizeObserver), with an ellipsis after the last. The edit input is one line and clips the same way.
- Phone Day schedule as a time grid was tight (14 hours at 24 px each only just fit the screen with the lane header): kept as it is on the AM page (22px rows or more, tested down to 320x568) with the AM/PM switch in a row of its own above it; PM is the night at the same row height (decisions of 2026-10-31, above).

## Data model implications

Resolved in `IMPLEMENTATION-SPEC.md` (typed contract: `packages/core/src/model.ts`), implemented 2026-10-30:

- Both is a view, not a list. Items carry `owners` (one list, or exactly the partner and the owner for a linked item that is ONE record). Custom lists are created in the picker.
- Per-person priority order (`slots` per owner), schedule entries with `start` and `length` (15-minute grid, default half an hour, minimum 15 minutes, inside a planner day of 7:00 to 7:00 the next morning), a per-list color, a household default Both color and each person's own Both color (an account preference, not in the store).
- Weekly and monthly text follows list selection: each list has its own text, one block per visible lane, the signed-in person first (spec 7.6).

- **Deleting an entry is a swipe, then a tap (the owner, 2026-11-01).** Swipe an entry to the left (a finger, a pen or a mouse; 28px or more, mostly sideways): its right end becomes a red Delete button; one tap deletes it (no second press); a tap elsewhere, Escape or a scroll puts it away. The keyboard: Delete or Backspace on the focused entry, then Enter. An entry being edited has no Delete (leave it first). The editor of an entry has no counter (no row-sized limit: the field stops at the server's 500 characters) and says a refused or stale save in the shell's banner. **Priorities in the Both view are cut in thirds like the schedule (the owner, 2026-11-01: "Add the third column click and hover for priorities as well")**: a blank line's left third is the left lane's priority, the middle third one priority for both of us, the right third the right lane's; the hover draws the cell a click would make in the color of whoever it is for. A line where one lane is taken has no thirds. **To-dos are cut in thirds the same way (the owner, 2026-11-01: "make todos have the same interface for left middle right being user 1 / both / user 2")**: a blank ruled line of the Day, Week or Month To-do, in the Both view, makes a to-do for the left lane's person (left third), one linked to-do (middle third) or the right lane's person (right third), opened in place over the line clicked, with the same hover ghost in the owner's color; one list shown has no thirds; the add field stays the keyboard's way. **The hover of a new entry or priority** is a softened copy of the real block in the owner's color (the owner: "make the hover more elegant looking and match user color").

- **0.9.0 (the owner, 2026-11-01):** the Month phone Priorities and Notes boxes are as tall as Week's; the entry being typed is "lifted" in its owner's tone (soft ring or glow, no underline, a small time chip, slim grips), drawn at its true size even for a quarter hour (0.12.0: the pill 0.9.0 typed it in is gone). Entry length and text, the thirds and the done pile are in their own notes above.
- **The phone's AM/PM and CalMind choices live in the hamburger menu (the owner, 2026-11-10: the row of two pills under the date "looks ridiculous"):** on a phone's Day Schedule tab the menu has a Schedule group at its foot: the view switch's own segmented AM | PM item (`aria-pressed`) and a CalMind events switch (the picker's knob switch, `role=switch`, `aria-checked`); choosing closes the menu, the focus returns to the hamburger, and a live message says what changed. The row is gone, so the lane names sit under the date bar and the schedule takes the height. The state stays on the page as a small dim AM or PM in the corner of the lane names over the hour labels (the hour labels' own size, no new control; screen readers get "Schedule, AM" from the section heading); CalMind's state is visible in the cards themselves and in the menu. The group is not in the menu on the Week, the Month, the To-do and Notes tabs or the Chores view. A desktop keeps both controls on the Schedule heading's line (no height cost, and its sidebar can be hidden, which would take them away). A Day opened from the Month now keeps the hamburger beside Back (Back follows it, both a little narrower), since the menu is the only way to AM/PM there. The menu is called "Menu" (Open menu / Close menu), not "dates".
