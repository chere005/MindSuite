Dev note. Every Day tab fits one screen with no scrolling. Restrict text to what displays properly on the smallest supported phone: characters per schedule slot and per priority, number and length of to-dos, and length of notes. Show a live counter at the limit (examples here: 6 to-dos, 400 notes characters; tune on a real device). Schedule is the default tab.

Phone: date selector, opened by tapping the date at the top

Dev note. The arrows step one day, week or month. Tapping the date opens the sheet: a calendar on Day, a Monday-to-Sunday list on Week, a month grid on Month. Picking closes the sheet. The round button is the list picker; the hamburger still opens the sidebar as a drawer.
