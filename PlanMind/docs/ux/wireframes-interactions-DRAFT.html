Interaction wireframes: on desktop, drag schedule entries in 15-minute steps, drag priorities and to-dos to reorder, double-click to edit; on a phone, press and hold to move, tap to edit

Desktop: Day panel, drag and edit states

Phone: press and hold to move, tap to edit

Dev notes

Move a schedule entry. Desktop: drag the block up or down. Phone: press and hold, then drag. It snaps to 15 minutes, shows its start and end while moving, auto-scrolls near the edge, and lands on the nearest free quarter-hour in its lane. Vertical only: dragging never changes the owner (use For). Esc, or dropping outside the grid, cancels.

Length. Entries now carry a start time in 15-minute steps and a length (minimum 30 minutes; the default is an hour since 2026-10-31). On desktop, drag the bottom edge to resize in 15-minute steps.

Reorder priorities and to-dos. Desktop: drag the row. Phone: press and hold, then drag. An accent line shows where it lands and numbers update on drop. Dragging one person's priority reorders only that person's list. A Both entry spans the lanes only while it sits on the same line in both lists; otherwise it shows as two purple half-lane cells.

Edit. Desktop: double-click edits in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels); a single click only selects. Phone: tap edits, with the For pills above the keyboard.

Drag mechanics. A drag starts after 4 px of movement; the phone hold is about 0.4 s, with a haptic tap on lift and on each 15-minute step. A drop is one save with the record's revision; if it is stale, the item snaps back and the list refreshes. The Day phone caps still apply.

Keyboard. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move 15 minutes (schedule) or one line (lists); Enter edits.
